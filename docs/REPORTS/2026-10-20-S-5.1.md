## What it does
- A played game's page title is the result, home team first, with the feed's score ("Jaguars 31, Texans 34: final score. Sunday, October 18."); og:title and description equal it, with no promise of conditions or where to watch. Scheduled, live and postponed titles are unchanged.
- One "Updated" stamp per game page, the later of the build's and the designations' poll, in the dateline's place (`gp_stamp`).
- The J-12 round: `gp_has_result(g)` (site_build.py) is true only when the feed marks the game `completed`, its state is post, it is not postponed and both scores are present. `gp_page_title` asks it, so a canceled game and a Final the feed gave no score for keep the pre-game title. `sbg_kicker` prints Final only for a completed game; a canceled game prints the feed's own word ("Canceled").
- The built-tree check (`gamepage_canary.check_final_pages`, called from `check_tree`) selects Final pages by `gp_has_result` on the build's own `/data/scoreboard.json` record, so it exempts exactly the pages the title exempts.

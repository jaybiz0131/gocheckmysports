## What it does
- `conditions_report.game_share_card` draws `share/games/<id>-square.png`, 1080 by 1080, on build for every game page (31 on the October 18 build), in the wall's palette (`WALL_PALETTE`), with GoCheckMySports and GoCheckMyWeather in plain text, no logos, no team colors.
- `site_build.game_share_row` builds the card from the record the page renders: matchup, kickoff in ET, network, score only once a game has started, the page's own state words in upper case (never "live"), the conditions plates or one plate saying why not ("LONDON · OUTSIDE NWS COVERAGE", "ROOF · USUALLY CLOSED", "DOME", "No forecast").
- The credit line prints only where the page prints one (`site_build.gp_credit`, shared by page and card), with its day when the reading is from another ET day.
- Game pages: og:image and twitter:image name the square card, og:image:width and height 1080, twitter:card summary. The 1200 by 630 `share/games/<id>.png` is still drawn; its URLs stay.

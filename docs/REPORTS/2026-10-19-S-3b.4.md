## What it does
- Team pages (NFL and college): a "Notify me" button and a one-line note, hidden until the browser supports push and the build is keyed. The device number is made on the first click and kept in localStorage.
- `netlify/functions/push.mjs`, POST /api/push: Origin allow list, 413 on a declared length over 8 KB and a bounded read, a push-host allow list (https, no port, no userinfo), 5,000 records at most. It stores endpoint, the two keys, up to eight teams, the hashed device number, created and updated in Netlify Blobs (`push-subs`), kept 180 days from last use.
- `netlify/functions/push-send.mjs`, hourly: one push per device per game between 7 PM and midnight ET on the Eastern day before kickoff, opening the game page, with kickoff in ET, network and the conditions line unless the reading is stale. Sent marks live per device in `push-sent` and expire 21 days after the last one; the mark is written before the send.
- Keyed only, through `WEB_PUSH_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `WEB_PUSH_VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` and `WEB_PUSH_SINCE` together: the button, `sw.js` push handlers, `/data/push-games.json`, and a Notifications section on privacy.html dated by `WEB_PUSH_SINCE`. Keyless, the site is byte-identical to main and both functions do nothing.
- No npm install: the Blobs client is vendored at `netlify/vendor/netlify-blobs.mjs` (@netlify/blobs 10.7.13 with @netlify/otel 6.0.6 and @netlify/runtime-utils 2.3.0, esbuild 0.28.2), rebuilt byte-identical by the reviewer, with `netlify/vendor/LICENSES.txt`. VAPID on node:crypto in `netlify/lib/webpush.mjs`.

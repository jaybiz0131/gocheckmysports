The scorecard: what nine out of ten means here
Ten lines, one point each, scored on production by the reviewer after each merge and by the strategist after each program. Nine out of ten means at most one line short, and lines 1 and 2 can never be the short one. A line nobody measured is unmet.

1. Truth. Every count and stamp from build_manifest.py; no game id twice; a scheduled game never prints Final; every stamp in ET with its zone; conditions only from the Weather Worker and never the feed's weather; a figure that cannot be read is left out and said ("No forecast", "Outside NWS coverage").
2. Fresh. No element rendered as current past its window: finals replace kickoffs; a kickoff more than four hours past is never tonight; the week rolls 6:00 AM ET Tuesday; a conditions reading past its age is muted with its age shown, never a stale number lit; a carriage row older than thirty days prints verify.
3. First screen. At 390, no tap: the next game's kickoff in ET, network and first stream, and its conditions plates; the wall at 1100 and up; the game page's title the search phrase and its first screen answering kickoff, where to watch and conditions.
4. Fast. The A-17 budgets in docs/HANDOFF.md section 5 held on every page they name; home HTML under 150 KB; Lighthouse mobile performance 90 or better on the home and a game page; no third host except the counter, the Weather Worker and the one beacon the October 14 ruling allows on game pages.
5. One look. The S-2d scoreboard look and nothing beside it: the token set only (a test fails on any other color), team colors never logos, no ballpark name or team mark in the style, Oswald from the pinned subset and no new font or script, nothing clipped at 390, AA contrast on every plate label, boards green in both themes.
6. Copy. No "live" until S-4 makes it true; no em dash; the September 16 copy law; the dateline above the headline; every stamp in words a reader understands.
7. Doors. SportsEvent and BroadcastEvent on every game page and SportsTeam on every team page, validated; canonical URLs; the sitemap index current; IndexNow after each merge that adds or changes pages; crawled-not-indexed read each Monday.
8. Money kept honest. Affiliate links only from data/affiliates.json where Jack holds one, plain links otherwise; the "how we make money" line wherever one renders; no ads, no picks, no email capture.
9. Operations. Every job in docs/HANDOFF.md with what it publishes and its spend; the one piece a day is the conditions report and nothing else publishes; builds inside the allowance; the count of record after every push; CI green on main.
10. Reliability. Canary green before every push; suite output to a file; zero console errors on the built home, a game page and a team page; every merged item with its report in docs/REPORTS/.
